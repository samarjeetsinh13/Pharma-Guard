Warfarin|Clopidogrel|MAJOR|Two blood-thinning mechanisms together raise bleeding risk.|Only together under specialist supervision.
Warfarin|Ciprofloxacin|MODERATE|The antibiotic can raise the INR (blood-thinning level).|Get the INR checked during and after the antibiotic course.
Warfarin|Azithromycin|MODERATE|May raise the INR and the chance of bleeding.|Monitor INR more often during the antibiotic course.
Warfarin|Amoxicillin|MODERATE|May slightly raise the INR.|Monitor INR if the antibiotic course is long.

# ---- Blood pressure and heart medicines ----

Atenolol|Salbutamol|MAJOR|The beta blocker blocks the inhaler and can trigger breathing difficulty.|Avoid in asthma; ask a doctor for an alternative.
Atenolol|Amlodipine|MINOR|Often prescribed together; combined effect can lower blood pressure a lot.|Monitor blood pressure and dizziness.
Atorvastatin|Amlodipine|MINOR|Small rise in statin level.|Usually no change needed.
